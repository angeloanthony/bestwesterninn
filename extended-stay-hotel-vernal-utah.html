Free breakfast every morning means one meal is already handled. On-site laundry means you don't need to haul clothes across town. And our front desk staff knows extended-stay guests by name.

Who Stays Extended at Best Western Vernal Inn

Our extended-stay guests include oilfield workers on 2-week and 4-week rotations flying into Vernal Regional Airport (just 0.6 miles away), pipeline and construction crews working seasonal projects in the Uintah Basin, government and BLM field staff on temporary assignments, families in between homes during relocation, and traveling nurses and healthcare workers stationed at Ashley Regional Medical Center. The common thread is people who need more than a hotel room — they need a temporary home.

  • Oilfield workers on rotating crew schedules

  • Government and BLM field personnel

  • Families relocating to the Uintah Basin
  • Seasonal workers — hunting guides, outdoor recreation staff
