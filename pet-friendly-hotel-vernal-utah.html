Dog-Friendly Things to Do Near the Hotel

Vernal is an outdoor town, and most of the public lands surrounding it are dog-friendly. Dogs on leash are welcome at Red Fleet State Park (12 miles north), on BLM lands throughout the Uintah Basin, and on most Ashley National Forest trails. Dinosaur National Monument has limited pet access — dogs are allowed in campgrounds and on paved areas but not on backcountry trails.

  • Red Fleet State Park — 12 miles, dogs on leash welcome
  • Ashley National Forest trails — 15 miles to the boundary
  • BLM public lands — open access throughout the basin
  • Vernal city parks — nearby green space for quick walks
  • Steinaker State Park — 7 miles, dogs on leash allowed
  • Dinosaur National Monument — limited access, campgrounds only

Pet Owner Tip: If you're visiting Red Fleet or Ashley National Forest with your dog, book a ground-floor kitchenette room. You'll have easy in-and-out access, space to prep dog food, and a fridge to store treats and water bottles.
