What to Do at Flaming Gorge

Flaming Gorge National Recreation Area stretches 91 miles along the Green River through dramatic red canyon walls. The reservoir is famous for trophy lake trout, kokanee salmon, and smallmouth bass fishing — some of the best coldwater fishing in the western United States. Beyond fishing, visitors come for boating, kayaking, swimming, and the stunning Red Canyon overlook, one of the most photographed viewpoints in Utah.

  • Trophy lake trout fishing — 30+ pounders caught regularly
  • Red Canyon Visitor Center — 1,400-foot canyon views
  • Flaming Gorge Dam — engineering marvel and tours available
  • Sheep Creek Geological Loop — scenic drive through time
  • Swim Beach and Cedar Springs Marina
  • Green River below the dam — fly fishing paradise
